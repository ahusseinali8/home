How I think about markets

Start with the world.
Test the investment case.

01 / The macro picture

Understand what is changing.

Study interest rates, inflation, policy and industry trends to place a company’s prospects in context.

02 / The business

Look beneath the headline.

Review earnings, margins, cash flow, valuation and management guidance. Compare the evidence with industry peers.

03 / The decision

Make uncertainty visible.

Test assumptions, consider alternative outcomes and think about sizing and risk. Data supports judgment; it does not remove uncertainty.
